# Evidence-first opportunity research

## Goal
Discover and validate consumer software problems across countries and sectors, then launch products that acquire and retain real users.

## Evidence model
Each claim should eventually include source platform, URL, observation date, language, country, problem statement, context, author-independent evidence count, and potential biases. Do not treat duplicate posts as independent demand.

## Sources
Reddit, YouTube, app stores, ecommerce reviews, community forums, public reports, search trends and competitor reviews. Use permitted APIs, official exports or explicitly authorized access; do not evade access controls or harvest private data.

## Funnel
1. Observe recurring user pain.
2. Cluster into problems and segment users.
3. Estimate reach and severity with explicit uncertainty.
4. Identify existing alternatives, pricing and customer acquisition channels.
5. Test an MVP with measurable activation and retention.
6. Validate willingness to pay and unit economics.
7. Iterate or stop based on evidence.

## Metrics
- Activation = activated users / signups
- Retention at day N = users active on day N / cohort users
- Conversion = paying customers / eligible users
- MRR = sum of monthly recurring subscription revenue
- CAC = acquisition spend / new customers acquired
- Gross margin = (revenue - direct delivery costs) / revenue

Avoid assuming users imply revenue: willingness to pay, retention, margins and acquisition cost matter.

## Current limitations
The initial scoring rubric is a subjective prioritization aid, not a statistical estimate. No source integrations or AI classification are live.
