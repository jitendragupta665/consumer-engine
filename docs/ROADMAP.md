# Roadmap

1. Evidence registry (local MVP)
2. Data-source adapters: Reddit, YouTube, app reviews, ecommerce reviews (permitted APIs/exports only)
3. Normalization, provenance, duplicate detection, multilingual classification
4. Market sizing, competitor research and willingness-to-pay validation
5. Opportunity ranking with uncertainty and transparent assumptions
6. MVP experiments, activation/retention tracking, monetization
7. Auth, background jobs, deployment, observability, CI/CD

No automatic collection or AI analysis is implemented yet.
